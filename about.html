Our journey

From a small workshop to a trusted manufacturer

Founded in 2001 with a clear vision to support India's growing logistics and manufacturing ecosystem, Ambica Patterns has steadily evolved into a dependable name in wooden pallets.

Over the years we've expanded our capabilities, adopted international standards and built long-term relationships across diverse sectors — combining traditional craftsmanship with modern manufacturing to deliver reliable, durable and cost-effective pallet solutions.

Every pallet type, built to spec
