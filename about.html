Our Journey

Founded in 2001 with a clear vision to support India's growing logistics and manufacturing ecosystem, Ambica Patterns India Pvt Ltd has steadily evolved into a dependable name in wooden pallets.

Over the years, we have expanded our capabilities, adopted international standards, and built long-term relationships with clients across diverse sectors. We combine traditional craftsmanship with modern manufacturing practices to deliver reliable, durable, and cost-effective pallet solutions.

What We Do

We specialize in the manufacturing of all types of wooden pallets, customized to meet specific client requirements. Our pallets are designed to ensure strength, safety, and efficiency across storage, handling, and transportation operations.

EPAL / Euro Pallets

Officially licensed European standard pallets for international shipping and automated systems.

Pinewood Pallets

Versatile and cost-effective solutions with excellent strength-to-weight ratio.

Export Pallets

ISPM-15 compliant pallets with heat treatment for international shipping.

Heavy-Duty Pallets

Reinforced construction for heavy machinery and industrial equipment.

Custom Pallets

Tailored to your exact specifications for specialized applications.

One-Way & Reusable

Solutions for both single-use exports and multi-cycle logistics needs.

Quality & Standards

Quality is at the core of everything we do. Our manufacturing processes follow strict quality control measures to ensure consistency and reliability.

We adhere to international packaging and export standards, including heat treatment and fumigation as required, ensuring our pallets are suitable for both domestic and global supply chains.

Our production facility is equipped with modern machinery and skilled manpower, enabling us to handle both bulk orders and customized requirements efficiently. From raw material selection to final inspection, every step is carefully monitored to maintain high standards.
