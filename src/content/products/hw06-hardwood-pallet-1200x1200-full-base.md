HW-06 is a fully double-faced hardwood pallet. The top deck of eight wide 22 mm boards, with two butted pairs either side of the centre, is mirrored by an identical bottom deck, so the pallet is reversible and spreads its load evenly whether it stands on the floor, on another pallet or on racking.

Between the two decks run four solid stringers, 100 mm high: 80 mm wide at the edges and a 60 mm pair close together in the centre to support the middle of the deck. Forklifts enter from the two open sides between the stringers. The pallet measures 1200 × 1200 mm and is 144 mm high.

A full bottom deck makes HW-06 the strongest of our square stringer pallets for stacking. ISPM-15 heat treatment is available for export.
