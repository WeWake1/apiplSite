PW-03 is a square pinewood pallet measuring 1120 × 1120 mm. A square deck suits drums, bulk bags and other round or square loads, and because all four sides are the same length it can be picked up from any direction without turning it.

Seven top boards (120 mm at the edges, 90 mm in between) sit on three 90 mm cross boards, flush with the ends, and nine 90 × 90 mm blocks. Three bottom boards run under the block rows. The pallet is 130 mm high, with generous fork openings on all four sides.

Like all our pinewood pallets, PW-03 can be built heavier for demanding loads and heat treated to ISPM-15 for export.
