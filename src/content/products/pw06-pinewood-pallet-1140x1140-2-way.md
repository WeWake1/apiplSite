PW-06 is a square stringer pallet. Instead of blocks, its deck of nine 110 mm boards rests on four solid stringers that run the full depth of the pallet: one at each edge and a pair close together in the centre, which carries the middle of the load where it is heaviest.

Three bottom boards, at both ends and across the centre, tie the stringers together and give a stable footprint. The pallet is built flush, with the deck boards ending level with the outer stringers, and stands 130 mm high. Forklifts enter from the two open sides between the stringers.

At 1140 × 1140 mm it matches the square container pallet footprint, so it suits drums and square containers where a stiff, economical pallet is needed. ISPM-15 heat treatment is available for export.
