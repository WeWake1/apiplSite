PW-08 is our 1100 × 1100 mm square block pallet with a feature borrowed from the Euro pallet: the outer bottom boards are chamfered along their upper outside edge between the blocks, with curved run-outs, so the wheels of a hand pallet truck ride over them smoothly from every side.

The deck has nine 22 mm top boards: at each edge a 127 mm and a 100 mm board are butted together, with five 97 mm boards between. They sit on three cross boards (120, 97 and 120 mm) and nine 80 mm blocks over a perimeter base. The pallet stands 146 mm high.

It is a practical choice for warehouses that move pallets mostly by hand pallet truck. Heat treatment to ISPM-15 is available for export.
